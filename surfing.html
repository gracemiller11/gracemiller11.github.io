All About Surfing

Surfing is a water sport where people ride ocean waves on a surfboard. It takes balance, practice, and patience to learn how to paddle, catch waves, and stand on a board. There are different types of surfboards, including longboards and shortboards, each designed for different styles of surfing. Popular surfing locations include Hawaii, California, and Australia. Before surfing, it is important to check ocean conditions, know how to swim, and follow beach safety rules. To learn more, visit What Is Surfing? and read about Types of Surfboards .
